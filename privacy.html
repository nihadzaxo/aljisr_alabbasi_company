    1. Who we are

    Al-Jisr Al-Abbasi Company (“we”, “us”) runs this website to present the LAVI products we distribute in Iraq and to make it easy for you to contact us. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the website and how we use the information you send us.

    2. Information we collect

    This website has no user accounts, takes no online payments and does not store the messages you type into it.

    • Messages you send us. When you contact us with a WhatsApp or phone button, your message is sent through WhatsApp or your phone. We receive what you choose to share — usually your name, phone number and message.
    • Language preference. Your browser remembers your chosen language on your own device. This setting is never sent to us.
    • Technical data handled by other services. To show fonts, 3D product views and the map, your browser connects to Google Fonts, jsDelivr and Google Maps. These services may receive your IP address and basic browser information.

    3. How we use your information

    We use the information you send us only to answer your questions, prepare and deliver your orders, share prices and availability, and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

    4. Third-party services

    When you contact us through WhatsApp, follow us on Instagram or TikTok, or use the embedded map, those services handle your data under their own privacy policies (WhatsApp and Instagram by Meta, TikTok, and Google for Fonts and Maps). We encourage you to read them.

    5. Cookies

    We do not set cookies ourselves. The embedded Google Map may set its own cookies when it loads.

    6. How long we keep information

    We keep order conversations for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, and then delete them.

    7. Your choices and rights

    You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us using the details below.

    8. Children

    This website is for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; orders are placed by adults and businesses.

    9. Security

    We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so please do not send us sensitive information such as card numbers.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page shows when it last changed.
